If you've heard scratching in your attic at night, or found half-eaten oranges under your tree in the morning, you've probably met the roof rat. It's the most common rat in Los Angeles neighborhoods, and it's a very good climber.

## How to tell if you have roof rats

Roof rats are slender, dark brown or black, with a tail longer than their body. You may never see one, because they're active at night. Watch for these signs instead:

- **Noises overhead at night** — scratching, scurrying, or gnawing in the attic or walls
- **Hollowed-out fruit** still hanging on the tree, or rinds on the ground
- **Droppings** — about half an inch long, pointed at the ends, often in the attic, garage, or behind appliances
- **Grease marks** along beams, pipes, and the edges of holes where they travel
- **Pets staring at walls or ceilings**

## Why LA yards are perfect for them

Roof rats like to live up high. In Los Angeles, they have everything they need:

**Food.** Citrus, avocado, and loquat trees are a buffet. So are pet food left outside, bird seed, and open trash.

**Cover.** Dense ivy, bougainvillea, and unpruned palm trees make great nesting spots.

**Highways.** Utility lines, fences, and branches touching the roof let rats travel from yard to yard — and straight onto your roof.

From the roof, they only need a gap about the size of a quarter to get into the attic. Common entry points include roof vents, gaps where pipes and wires enter, and openings at the eaves.

## What you can do this weekend

1. **Pick up fallen fruit** every day, and harvest ripe fruit promptly.
2. **Trim branches** so they're at least three to four feet from the roof.
3. **Thin out ivy** and other dense ground cover near the house.
4. **Store pet food and bird seed** in sealed containers, and bring pet bowls in at night.
5. **Check vents and screens** for holes or damage.

## Why traps alone usually aren't enough

Trapping a few rats in the attic helps, but if the entry points are still open, more rats will move in. Lasting rodent control has three parts: **remove the rats that are inside, seal the ways in, and monitor** to make sure no new ones show up.
